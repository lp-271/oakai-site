What We Build

Every part of the front desk, automated.

Oak AI handles the calls, texts, and follow-ups that eat up your day — so your team can focus on the customer in front of them.

AI Voice Agents

A natural-sounding assistant answers every call, 24/7 — handling questions, taking messages, and booking appointments without hold music.

AI SMS Assistant

Customers text your business number and get real answers back — instantly, in plain language, with no app to download.

Appointment Booking Automation

Calls and texts turn straight into confirmed appointments on your calendar — no double-booking, no manual entry.

Google Review Follow-Up

After a visit, customers get a friendly text nudging them to leave a Google review — automatically, at the right moment.

QR Code Opt-In Systems

In-store QR codes let customers opt in to texts with one scan — fully compliant, no clipboard sign-up sheets required.
